Chief of Security — what the two records add up to.

The product's record and the lab's record are different machines reading different material; the chief reads both and refuses to say things they do not establish. Behaviour is judged against a baseline, and a baseline needs minutes of the same scene — one animal crossing on one day is not a pattern, it is a sighting. Where the two records disagree the chief says so.

reading the records…
What "Chief of Security" is not. It is not a model with opinions about footage. It is deterministic arithmetic on counts the two records already hold, with every claim carrying the line it came from. A suspicion is not an accusation: one animal is one animal, and one minute is one minute, until the record has more than one of them.

This page is the LAB's chief. The product has its own, and it is the one that runs a site: the console's Chief, at /v1/chief. That one reads the product's own record — the attention queue, the shift hand-over, and what changed since you last looked — and it names what it does not know. This page reads the two lab records, and exists to show the arithmetic before there is anything to judge.
